FEFFERMAN NSE CLASSIFICATION -- SEMANTIC CONTRACT

This is a Lean 4 skeleton with explicit formulas and conditional composition
proofs. Analysis is an uninstantiated signature. This package does not construct
the real numbers, derivatives, Lebesgue integrals, or an NSE solution.

REALIZATION REQUIRED
Scalar = the real numbers; zero/one/add/mul/neg/le/lt have their usual meanings.
Space = Fin 3 -> real, with Euclidean norm norm3.
dx is the coordinate partial derivative; dt is the time derivative within
[0,infinity). Smoothness is C-infinity, including t=0 via smooth local extension.
squareIntegrable v means integrability of ||v||^2 with respect to three-
dimensional Lebesgue measure. integralNormSq v is that integral when finite.
No theorem asserts that an arbitrary Analysis value has these properties.
An actual mathlib realization and identification proofs are still required.

All field values for t < 0 are representational extensions; the physical
predicates quantify only over t >= 0. The derivative at t=0 uses the right-hand
domain, so arbitrary negative-time values must not affect its interpretation.

CLAUSE CONVENTIONS
01 momentum; 02 incompressibility; 03 initial trace.
04 every spatial derivative of the initial velocity decays faster than every
   polynomial. 05 every mixed force derivative has joint space/time decay.
06 velocity AND pressure are smooth on the closed positive time half-space.
07 one finite energy bound C works at every t >= 0. Finiteness is explicit.
08 spatial unit periodicity of initial velocity and force.
09 every mixed force derivative has rapid time decay uniformly in space.
10 spatial unit periodicity of solution velocity.
11 smooth solution velocity and pressure, as in 06.
The official appended erratum additionally requires periodic pressure.

Decay is written in weighted form, with natural multi-indices and powers:
norm(derivative) * weight^K <= C. The positive weights make this equivalent
to the negative-power form after the real-analysis realization. The constants
may depend on the derivative orders, K, and the chosen data, not on x or t.

A/B require force identically zero and universal admissible initial data.
C/D existentially choose admissible initial data AND admissible force.
C/D assert there is no qualifying global pair (pressure,velocity).
They are not defined as failure of one trajectory or of a numerical method.
The torus is represented by periodic lifts on real three-space. No zero-mean
condition, pressure gauge normalization, or whole-space energy bound is added
to B/D. Both velocity and pressure are periodic.

Each StatementAt fixes one positive viscosity. Statement quantifies over all
positive viscosities. No normalization to nu=1 is assumed. Failure of the
universal A yields C-at-some-viscosity, not C-for-every-viscosity by logic alone.

PROOF STATUS
No global research axiom declarations or proof-hole commands are present.
Missing mathematics is expressed as record fields or theorem parameters.
A zero axiom count does NOT mean those parameters have been supplied.
The route observables are predicates awaiting genuine mathematical definitions.
The Rocq proofs are not imported into Lean and are not automatically trusted.
Only a successful run on the target Lean compiler establishes that the skeleton
elaborates and its conditional proofs are kernel checked.

An instance for a toy Scalar would only classify that toy interpretation.
It would not establish a Fefferman statement about the real NSE.

MASTER-THEOREM LAYER (v0.2.0)
regularForceAt means the force is admissible and EVERY admissible initial
datum has at least one qualifying global solution.
breakdownForceAt means the force is admissible and SOME admissible initial
datum has NO qualifying global solution. No uniqueness premise is added.
The exact semantic alternatives are classically exhaustive and disjoint.

ForceRegimes contains proposed certificate criteria, not analytic proofs.
ForceRegimeSoundness contains the two required semantic implication proofs.
unresolvedForceAt is relative to the criteria and asserts neither certificate.
Certificate trichotomy is classical; it is not a computational classification.

DomainMasterCore adds zero-force regular membership for every viscosity.
DomainMaster additionally requires a certified breakdown force at each viscosity.
MasterTheoremA is the whole-space DomainMaster. MasterTheoremB is the periodic
DomainMaster. Neither is instantiated. Their conditional conjunction proofs
are not proofs of the hypotheses or of an actual real NSE classification.

Full regular-force coverage instead excludes breakdown at a fixed viscosity.
It is incompatible with the positive-breakdown master at that viscosity.
An infinite-time unbounded critical observable alone is not identified with
finite-time singularity or nonexistence of a qualifying Fefferman solution.

FINITE GEOMETRY (v0.6.1)
The finite carrier is Nat -> Scalar, paired over i<n; tails are ignored.
Radius nonnegativity is required only below n. The same scalar structure is
used before and after transport. SourceDifference contains ordinary pairing
laws and has a constructed finite-coordinate implementation. Isometry does
not assert equality of private physical trajectories or continuum limits.

MASTER AC — R3 INTERNAL A/C MASTER LAYER
=======================================

MasterAC is the internal theorem surface.  It contains only:

  1. zero force satisfies the internal R3 regular criterion for every positive
     viscosity;
  2. every positive viscosity has an internal breakdown object satisfying the
     model's own breakdown criterion.

MasterAC contains no Fefferman A/C statement, no hasGlobalSolution statement,
no nonexistence-of-solution statement, and no assertion that arbitrary internal
forces are Fefferman forces.

MasterACProjection is deliberately weaker than a total force identification.
It identifies only:

  * the zero force needed by A;
  * the particular MasterAC breakdown witness family needed by C.

All other forces may remain unidentified or unresolved without affecting the
final A/C conversion theorem.

The projection also carries the same-physical-input realization/exit ports:
internal regularity is realized by an R3 storage witness on the same ForcedInput
and exits through critical control; internal breakdown is realized by a
NetWorkWitness on the same ForcedInput and every qualifying global solution
would bound its mass ledger.  The scalar net-work theorem then excludes such a
global solution.

Primary theorem
---------------

  MASTER_AC_IMPLIES_FEFFERMAN_A_AND_C

  LedgerArithmetic a ->
  R3DerivationModel a ->
  MasterAC m ->
  MasterACProjection m ->
  A a ∧ C a.

This file therefore separates three levels cleanly:

  INTERNAL THEOREM      MasterAC
  PHYSICAL PROJECTION   MasterACProjection
  OFFICIAL OUTPUT       Fefferman A ∧ C

The future concrete Leray-Hopf embedding belongs in the projection layer.  It
can prove the required identification/reflection properties without changing
MasterAC itself.

This release does not claim that the internal breakdown witness family or the
concrete real-analysis/Leray-Hopf projection has already been constructed.
Those are explicit frontier inputs at the correct boundary rather than A/C
restatements.
